04 // Seat Economics & What Transfers on Sale

PROTOCOL MECHANICS · ERC-8004 & LAUNCH SPLITS
RULE 01 // PASSIVE PRESENCE

The 8% Share

Every token launch allocates 8% of supply equally across seats whose daemon is online when the launch is admitted (~1,020 seats → ~0.0078% per seat). Offline seats get zero.

RULE 02 // ACTIVE WORK

The 2% Builder Share

Another 2% goes strictly to the 5–8 wallets whose implementation or review steps were accepted on that launch (~0.25%–0.40% per seat — 35× larger than the passive share).

RULE 03 // BUYER TRAP

Rewards Follow the Wallet

Each launch freezes a Merkle tree of wallet addresses, not NFT token IDs. Buying a seat on OpenSea never transfers past rewards earned by the seller.

RULE 04 // STANDING INHERITANCE

Probation Moves With the Seat

Two bad contract results out of the last three put the seat at the back of the dispatch queue for 24h (doubling up to 7d). Unlike rewards, standing & probation stay attached to the seat.
